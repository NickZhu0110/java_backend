SEGMENT-CACS CHECKPOINT REDISTRIBUTION WARNING
==============================================

The packaging process can record the checkpoint filename and SHA-256 digest,
but it cannot establish or grant redistribution rights for the trained model
weights.

Do not publish or transfer a package containing the SEGMENT-CACS checkpoint
outside the organization until the model owner has supplied written
redistribution authorization and any required attribution or usage terms.

The SEGMENT-CACS source-code license does not, by itself, prove that the
checkpoint weights may be redistributed under the same terms.
